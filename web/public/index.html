Map in a Box Web

Explore the world by ear. Move across the map with the arrow keys, or on a phone or tablet by tilting it. Map in a Box tells you the country, city or ocean you are over, and plays a sound for each country. Street mode takes you down to the streets of a town, with the places along them.

It works with NVDA, JAWS, Narrator, VoiceOver and TalkBack, or with the browser's own voice. Headphones help you hear the tones move from left to right.

Lay it flat, screen up: flat is still. Tip it well over, more than 60 degrees, to move: the top edge down (away from you) for north, up (towards you) for south, the right edge down for east, the left edge down for west. Hold the tilt to keep going, and lay it flat to stop. The first time, a short practice teaches the four tilts.

Journey planner

The Journey planner button, or the Journey planner section under the map, plans a trip with Rome2Rio: flights, trains, buses, ferries and driving. Type where you are going, and where from (leave From empty to start where you are on the map), then choose Plan journey. Swipe up or down on Option (or use the arrow keys) to hear each way, with its time and price; choose Show steps to go through its legs one by one, or Read all to hear the whole plan. For a flight, the flights on the travel date are listed, non-stop first. Rome2Rio's times and prices are estimates, not timetables.

Find food along the way lists cafés, restaurants, take-aways, pubs and bakeries from OpenStreetMap in journey order: within 100 metres of the road for drive, bus and walk legs, and near each station, airport and stop. Choose one to hear it on the map, open it in Apple Maps, or search for it on Uber Eats or DoorDash.

Steering (phones and tablets)

Turn on Steering and lay the phone flat, screen up. When it says Ready, flat is still. Tip it well over, more than 60 degrees, to move, like rolling a marble on a tray: the top edge down, away from you, for north; the top edge up, towards you, for south; the right edge down for east; the left edge down for west. Each tilt moves once, with a tone from that side; hold the tilt to keep moving. Bring it back towards flat (below about 30 degrees) to stop, with a soft click. A tilt halfway between two directions does nothing. Steering only pauses if you tip it past upright. Shake the phone firmly to turn steering off, and shake it again to turn it back on.

Each tilt moves lets you choose how far a tilt goes: one step, the next town or suburb, or the next country on the world map; one step or the next corner in street mode. Practise steering runs the short practice again.

In street mode, the Steering: pick turns or free button (also in Settings) chooses how steering moves. Pick turns follows the street. At a corner it stops; lay the phone flat, then tilt towards a way to hear it, for example "Turn left onto Ann Street, heading west", and hold the tilt to take it. Free goes straight the way you tilt, across blocks too.

Some browsers keep F6 for themselves. If F6 does nothing, use I.
