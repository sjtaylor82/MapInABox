Map in a Box Web

Explore the world by ear. Move across the map with the arrow keys, or on a phone or tablet by tilting it. Map in a Box tells you the country, city or ocean you are over, and plays a sound for each country. Street mode takes you down to the streets of a town, with the places along them.

It works with NVDA, JAWS, Narrator, VoiceOver and TalkBack, or with the browser's own voice. Headphones help you hear the tones move from left to right.

          Journey planner

          The Journey planner button, or the Journey planner section under the map, plans a trip with Rome2Rio: flights, trains, buses, ferries and driving. Type where you are going, and where from (leave From empty to start where you are on the map), then choose Plan journey. Swipe up or down on Option (or use the arrow keys) to hear each way, with its time and price; choose Show steps to go through its legs one by one, or Read all to hear the whole plan. For a flight, the flights on the travel date are listed, non-stop first. Rome2Rio's times and prices are estimates, not timetables.

          Find food along the way lists food venues from OpenStreetMap in journey order: within 100 metres of the road for drive, bus and walk legs, and near each station, airport and stop. Choose one to hear it on the map, open it in Apple Maps, or search for it on Uber Eats or DoorDash.
