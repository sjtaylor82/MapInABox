Map in a Box Web

Explore the world by ear. Move across the map with the arrow keys, or on a phone or tablet by tilting it. Map in a Box tells you the country, city or ocean you are over, and plays a sound for each country. Street mode takes you down to the streets of a town, with the places along them.

It works with NVDA, JAWS, Narrator, VoiceOver and TalkBack, or with the browser's own voice. Headphones help you hear the tones move from left to right.

It works like the arrow keys. Hold it steady at a comfortable angle, screen towards you, upright or sideways; when it says Ready, that angle is still. Tilt it firmly one way to move: top edge away for north, towards you for south, lower the right side for east, the left side for west. A tone plays from that side. Come back to still before the next move, or hold the tilt to keep going. The first time, a short practice teaches the four tilts.

Steering (phones and tablets)

Tilt steering works like the arrow keys. Turn on Steering, then hold the device steady at a comfortable angle, screen towards you, upright or sideways. When it says Ready, that angle counts as still. Tilt it firmly one way to move once: top edge away from you for north, towards you for south, lower the right side for east, lower the left side for west. A tone plays from that side: right for east, left for west, higher for north, lower for south. Come back to still (a soft click) before the next move, or hold the tilt to keep moving. A tilt halfway between two directions does nothing. Turn it right over, or put it down at a very different angle, and steering pauses; once you hold it steady again, that angle counts as still, which is how to reset it.

Each tilt moves lets you choose how far a tilt goes: one step, the next town or suburb, or the next country on the world map; one step or the next corner in street mode. Practise tilting runs the short practice again.

In street mode, the Steering: pick turns or free button (also in Settings) chooses how steering moves. Pick turns follows the street. At a corner it stops; come back to still, then tilt towards the way you want to hear it, for example "Turn left onto Ann Street", and hold the tilt to take it. Free goes straight the way you tilt, across blocks too.

Some browsers keep F6 for themselves. If F6 does nothing, use I.
