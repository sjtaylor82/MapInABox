Map in a Box Web

Explore the world by ear. Move across the map with the arrow keys, or on a phone or tablet by tilting it. Map in a Box tells you the country, city or ocean you are over, and plays a sound for each country.

It works with NVDA, JAWS, Narrator, VoiceOver and TalkBack, or with the browser's own voice. Headphones help you hear the tones move from left to right.

Hold it comfortably, then tilt forward, back, left or right to move. The further you tilt, the faster you go. Tap the map to make the way you are holding it level again.
