Bundled and downloaded data
---------------------------

- The bundled world-city database is a transformed and merged dataset derived
  from the SimpleMaps Basic World Cities Database and the GeoNames cities1000
  gazetteer extract. Both sources are licensed under Creative Commons
  Attribution 4.0 International.
  https://simplemaps.com/data/world-cities
  https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/
- Other GeoNames geographical features: licensed under Creative Commons
  Attribution 4.0 International. https://www.geonames.org/
- The bundled offline postcode dataset is derived from the GeoNames postal
  code export, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
  International. For Canada, the Netherlands, and the United Kingdom this
  data is limited to the first part of the postcode only.
  https://download.geonames.org/export/zip/
- Natural Earth country boundaries: public-domain Natural Earth data.
  https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
- OurAirports airport data: released to the public domain.
  https://ourairports.com/data/
- OpenStreetMap data obtained while using the app: Copyright OpenStreetMap
  contributors, available under the Open Database License 1.0.
  https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
- GTFS feeds downloaded by the user retain the licence and terms specified by
  their individual transport agencies and feed publishers.
- MobilityDatabase feed-catalogue metadata is downloaded from MobilityData.
  Individual feeds retain the licences and terms specified by their respective
  publishers. https://mobilitydatabase.org/
- Australian G-NAF results retain the applicable source-data attribution and
  licensing terms of Geoscape Australia and the relevant government sources.
